3. RESOURCES (tables: resource, resourcefeature, resourceavailability,
   maintenanceperiod, resourcecategory)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

What is VERIFIED is only that each kind of facility exists in that building:

  Newman group study rooms (4th floor) .................... [S1][S2]
  Torgersen atrium study court ............................ [S3]
  McComas basketball/volleyball courts, pool, studios ..... [S4][S5]
  War Memorial racquetball/squash courts, badminton/
    pickleball layouts, table tennis, yoga studio ......... [S6][S7]
  Squires meeting rooms ................................... [S8]

Everything else is SIMULATED:
  - Resource names such as "Newman Group Study Room 4", "Torgersen Project
    Room 1", or "Squires Meeting Room A". These are CampusConnect labels,
    not official Virginia Tech room numbers or names.
  - Torgersen Project Rooms 1-2 are fictional. VT does not list them as
    reservable spaces.
  - Capacities, required_credits, max_reservation_time, descriptions,
    and location_description text.
  - Resource location: inherited from the selected building. Building
    latitude/longitude come from the building's official coordinates; room-level
    positions are not verified.
  - Resource features (whiteboards, displays, projectors, nets, mats).
    The only exception is "Power station" in Newman rooms, which follows
    [S2].
  - Availability windows, the Fall 2026 range 2026-08-17 to 2026-12-18,
    and the hours: Newman 08:00-23:00, Torgersen 08:00-22:00, RecSports
    07:00-22:00, Squires weekdays 09:00-21:00. These are NOT official VT
    operating hours.
  - All six maintenance periods and their reasons.
  - Resource categories (names and descriptions).
  - Owner accounts. Resources belong to fictional staff accounts, not to
    real VT departments or employees.

The 2-hour max_reservation_time on Newman study rooms mirrors VT's
2-hours-per-day rule [S2]. CampusConnect applies it per reservation, not
per person per day, so it is an approximation, not VT policy.

Real reservations at these facilities go through VT's own systems
(bookings.lib.vt.edu for Libraries [S2], the RecSports reservations page
[S5]). CampusConnect data does not represent those systems.

5. CREDIT, CANCELLATION, AND REFUND RULES (ASSUMPTIONS)
-------------------------------------------------------
The Phase 3 documentation defines credit columns but no credit, refund, or
cancellation policy. seed.sql uses these ASSUMED rules:

  a. New account: +20 credits (transaction_type 'initial_grant').
  b. Monthly reset on the 1st at 00:00: the balance returns to 20 and
     unused credits do not roll over. A 'monthly_reset' row stores the
     top-up amount and is only written when the balance changes;
     credit_last_reset is still updated. Suspended accounts do not
     receive resets.
  c. Booking deducts the resource's required_credits once, as a flat
     charge per reservation, not per hour ('reservation_charge').
  d. A user who cancels at least 24 hours before the start gets a full
     refund ('refund'). Cancelling less than 24 hours ahead gets no refund.
  e. A reservation cancelled because of maintenance is fully refunded.
  f. No-shows are not refunded.
  g. Transaction amounts are signed: credits added are positive,
     deductions negative. So for every user,
     credit_balance = SUM(credittransaction.amount).
  h. Credit amounts and prices are fictional and are not Virginia Tech
     prices or fees.


6. SIMULATED ACTIVITY
---------------------

Reservations: 55 total.
  - 38 completed, 11 confirmed (upcoming), 4 cancelled, 2 no_show.
  - Status meanings: 'confirmed' = upcoming; 'completed' = used;
    'cancelled' = cancelled by the user or due to maintenance;
    'no_show' = past and not used.
  - A reservation that starts after the snapshot is 'confirmed'.

Generation constraints (all checked when the data was produced):
  - Active reservations do not overlap on the same resource.
  - Active reservations do not overlap a maintenance period.
  - Every reservation fits inside an availability window.
  - No reservation exceeds max_reservation_time.
  - Each reservation is created after the user's account and before its
    start time.
  - No balance ever goes negative.

The four main journeys:
  A  maya_r   Books Newman Group Study Room 4, completes it, leaves a
              5-star resource review and a review of the owner; has an
              upcoming booking.
  B  liam_k   Books Squires Meeting Room A, cancels 3 days ahead, and is
              refunded in full.
  C  priya_s  Uses six resources across four buildings over seven weeks,
              with three resource reviews and one reservation review.
  D  diego_m  Pool lane booking cancelled by scheduled maintenance and
              refunded; rebooks, completes, then plays squash.

Reviews:
  - ResourceReview: written only by users who completed a reservation of
    that resource, after it ended.
  - ReservationReview: only for completed reservations. The reserving
    student is the reviewer and the resource owner is the reviewee
    (ASSUMPTION; see section 7).
  - All review text is fictional.

Notifications:
  - Generated from the same timeline: welcome, credit reset, booking
    confirmation, cancellation (with refund outcome), no-show, and account
    suspension.

Events the schema cannot store: no column records when or why a
reservation was cancelled, when a maintenance period was announced, or
why a user was banned. In seed.sql these facts appear only in the timing
of credit transactions and notifications, and in the notification
message text.


7. LIMITATIONS AND OPEN QUESTIONS
---------------------------------
- Credit, refund, and cancellation rules (section 5) are not in the
  Phase 3 documents and need team confirmation.
- ReservationReview direction: the schema stores one reviewer and one
  reviewee per reservation but does not say who reviews whom. We chose
  reserving student -> resource owner. The schema allows only one
  ReservationReview per reservation, so two-way reviews are impossible.
- Allowed values of role, reservation status, image status,
  transaction_type, and notification_type are not documented. The values
  used here are our choices.
- Timestamps are timestamp without time zone and are interpreted as
  Blacksburg local time.
- The DBML spells Resource.descripton; schema.sql (and this seed) use
  "description".
- Building coordinates are VT's building-level points. Room coordinates
  are not available.
- Monthly resets are stored as precomputed rows. No database job performs
  them.
